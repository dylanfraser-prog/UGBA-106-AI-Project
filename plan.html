MyChart AI — Marketing Strategy
Solution Design
What MyChart AI Does
Cross-System Record Synthesis
Pulls records from TEFCA, Carequality, and CommonWell at point of care. Merges them into a single coherent patient view — no hunting required.
↑ 90 min saved/provider/day
AI Chart Drafting
Ambient documentation via Epic Art generates draft notes from the encounter. Physician reviews, edits, and signs — not types from scratch.
1M+ drafts/month live now
Risk Pattern Detection
Cosmos CoMET models trained on 118M patients flag longitudinal risk signals — gradual A1C rise, BP trending, kidney function changes — before they become diagnoses.
118M patient training set
Emmie Patient Concierge
Translates clinical visits into plain language. Answers patient questions, confirms next steps, and surfaces care gaps — 24/7 without provider involvement.
Natural language, HIPAA-compliant

Launch Roadmap
Phase 1

Deploy within 2–3 UC Health sites. Instrument physician time savings and patient engagement metrics. Build case study assets.
Phase 2
Physician Champion Program
Identify 10–15 early adopters across specialties. Co-create content, testimonials, and CME curriculum. Build word-of-mouth in clinical networks.
Phase 3
Patient-Facing Launch
Emmie AI activated in MyChart app for enrolled patients. In-app onboarding, post-visit email sequence, care checklist rollout.
Phase 4
Health System Expansion
ROI deck, trade press campaign, Epic UGM presentation. Target CIO/CMO at 20 Riverside / SoCal health systems using existing TEFCA connections.
